My name is Vy. I was student at Ronald Reagan High School. I am infatuated with creating different forms of art with varied mediums that I explore.

For years now, I have been enjoying trying new forms of art, be it 2-dimensional or 3-dimensional.

I take an imaginative approach to my art; typically using multiple mediums and a variety of movements to create my work.

I aspire to produce art that makes people a little uncomfortable and forces them to create their own narrative.

I am currently a student at UW Madison. I continue my art practice and I am working on developing a stronger sense of self in my art.
